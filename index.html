Investment Philosophy

"Monetise Time"

Time is the most scarce and irreversible resource in investing. My philosophy is built on the conviction that patient, systematic allocation — anchored in probability and structural trend — unlocks compounding returns that short-term trading cannot replicate.

01
📈
Assume Uptrend in Mid to Long Term

Capital markets have historically rewarded patient holders. Rather than second-guessing short-term noise, I orient positions around the structural upward bias of equities and risk assets over multi-year horizons. Time in the market consistently outperforms timing the market.

02
🎯
Risk Control by Probability

Every position is sized and managed through a probabilistic lens. Expected value, asymmetry of outcomes, and scenario-weighted downside guide both entry and exit decisions. Preservation of capital is the prerequisite to compounding — losses destroy time, the most valuable resource.

03
🔬
Fundamental & Technical

Fundamentals define the destination — earnings power, valuation, and competitive moat determine where an asset should trade. Technicals define the timing — price action, momentum, and market structure determine the optimal moment to act.
